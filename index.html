Official Binho rules

Straight from Binho — these govern standard play, and everything in the season rulebook sits on top of them.

The game

  • Ball starts at center. First shooter is settled by rock paper scissors or an arm wrestle — though the winner of the previous match shoots first if they're still on the board.
  • Players alternate flicks, each one taken from wherever the ball came to a complete stop.
  • In 2v2 the order alternates across teams: team 1 player 1, team 2 player 1, team 1 player 2, team 2 player 2.
  • First to 7 goals wins.
  • The ball has to fully cross the goal line — you should see turf behind it. Bouncing back out still counts.
  • Whoever conceded kicks off from center.

Cards

  • Yellow — opponent gets a free kick from center field.
  • Second yellow becomes a red.
  • Red — opponent takes a penalty from anywhere behind midfield and you lose a player. Which one is decided by a card flip: the opponent flips, the offender calls red or yellow, and the winner picks the piece that comes off. Cards reset afterward.

What earns a card

  • Touching or double-touching the ball before your shot — yellow, or red inside your own box.
  • Fingering the crossbar on any shot — automatic red.
  • Putting the ball out of bounds — yellow from open play, red from inside your own box.
  • Moving the board mid-shot — yellow, or red inside your own box.
  • Your head can't cross the midfield line during a shot.
Season note: under our house rules an own goal also costs you a player. Standard Binho games follow the official version above.

Good to know

  • When the ball sits against the bands you may flick from behind them, fingers technically out of bounds.
  • Against a sideline or baseline, only your shooting hand may pull the band back.
